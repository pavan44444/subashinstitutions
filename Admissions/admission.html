Programmes and eligibility

B.Sc. Nursing

4 years plus 1 year of internship

PUC / 10+2 or equivalent with a minimum of 45% in Physics, Chemistry and Biology. Age 17 by 31st December of the year of admission.

M.Sc. Nursing

2 years

A pass in B.Sc. Nursing with a minimum of 55%, registration as a Registered Nurse and Registered Midwife, and a minimum of one year of experience.

PB B.Sc. Nursing

2 years

A pass in General Nursing & Midwifery (GNM), and registration as a nurse.

GNM Nursing

3 years 6 months

PUC / PDC / 10+2 or an equivalent examination. Age 17 by 31st December of the year of admission.

Diploma in Pharmacy

10+2 or equivalent with Science subjects, a minimum of 50% aggregate and a minimum age of 17 years.

Completed 12th standard from a recognised board.
