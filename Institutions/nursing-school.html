Subash School of Nursing

A 3 year 6 month nursing diploma programme, recognised by the Indian Nursing Council and the Karnataka Nursing Council.

Duration: 3 Years 6 Months Medium: English 6 months internship

About the school

Nursing is the process of caring for people’s lives by providing essential services to humankind. Nursing courses teach the professional way of caring for and serving people. The impact of nursing is felt throughout life, and it is a service that is needed at all times.

Eligibility

The minimum qualification is Pre-University Exam / PDC / 10+2 pass or an equivalent exam, in Science, Arts or Commerce.

Male and female candidates with good character, conduct and sound health, between the ages of 17 and 35 years.

Affiliated

Karnataka State Diploma in Nursing Examination Board (KSDNEB)

Recognition

Indian Nursing Council, New Delhi

Karnataka Nursing Council, Bangalore

Duration

3 years 6 months

Medium of instruction

English

Internal assessment

Each subject has internal assessment marks. These are given on the basis of monthly assignments or trimester examinations, personal grooming, conduct, attendance and professional attitude.

Documents required

  • Original SSLC and PUC / 10+2 marks card
  • Transfer certificate
  • Conduct certificate
  • Migration certificate
  • 10 passport size photos

Examinations

  • Three public examinations are held by the Government of Karnataka. Written and practical examinations are conducted every year.
  • An internship training of 6 months follows at the end of the academic course.
  • Candidates with attendance below 75% are not allowed to appear for the examinations.
  • Classroom performance, clinical attendance, general conduct and performance in the clinical area are considered when deciding whether a candidate may appear for the examinations.
