Subash College of Nursing

A four-year nursing degree programme affiliated to Rajiv Gandhi University of Health Sciences, Bangalore.

Duration: 4 Years Medium: English Affiliated to RGUHS
Eligibility

The minimum qualification is Pre-University Exam / PDC / 10+2 pass in Science, with a minimum of 50% marks.

Male and female candidates with good character, conduct and sound health, between the ages of 17 and 35 years.

Affiliated

Rajiv Gandhi University of Health Sciences, Bangalore

Recognition

Indian Nursing Council, New Delhi

Karnataka Nursing Council, Bangalore

Duration

4 years

Medium of instruction

English

Internal assessment

Each subject has internal assessment marks. These are given on the basis of monthly assignments or trimester examinations, personal grooming, conduct, attendance and professional attitude.

Examinations

  • Four public examinations are held by Rajiv Gandhi University of Health Sciences. Both written and practical examinations are conducted every year.
  • Candidates with attendance below 75% are not allowed to appear for the examinations.
  • Classroom performance, clinical attendance, general conduct and performance in the clinical areas are considered when deciding whether a candidate may appear for the examinations.
