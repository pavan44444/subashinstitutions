About the college

Pharmacy is the medical field that deals with the preparation, distribution and appropriate use of medicines and drugs for therapeutic purposes. Subash College of Pharmacy offers a Diploma in Pharmacy for students who want to start a career in this field.

Pharmacists link the doctor’s prescription with the patient’s use of medicine, and they also work with scientists and doctors on research and new pharmaceutical practices.

Why choose pharmacy?

💊

A growing field

The need for effective medicines and immunisation keeps creating opportunities in pharmacy.

🤝

Patient-centred care

Pharmacists connect prescriptions with patients and help them use medicines properly.

🔬

Flexible careers

From nuclear pharmacy to veterinary pharmacy, there are many directions to explore.

Advancing your career

After the diploma, students can move on to a bachelor’s or master’s degree in pharmacy. Bachelor’s options include:

  • Bachelor of Pharmacy (B.Pharm)
  • B.Pharm (Hons.)
  • Bachelor of Pharmacy in Ayurveda
