About the hospital

Subash Medical Centre is a multi-specialty hospital with 100 beds, serving for a decade with well-equipped facilities. It is on the Bangalore–Mysore Highway and meets critical needs around the clock with care.

Nearby clinics and government hospitals at Ramanagaram are also connected with the hospital for all critical medical service requirements.
