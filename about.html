Who we are

Subash Group of Institutions is led by Dr. K. Subash, MBBS, who has vast experience and professional competence in the field of health. He is supported by Mrs. N.R. Jayanthi, M.Sc., the honorable secretary, Dr. S. Madanika, MBBS, MS (OBG) and Dr. S. Darshan Deep, MBBS.

With the support of the Holy Cross Education Trust, the institution manages its nursing programmes and the day-to-day affairs of students and staff. The objectives set by the trust are carried out effectively and given the right direction.

We aim to add a systematic and value-based education to every programme we offer.
