We train people who stay calm when it matters.

Welcome to Subash Group of Institutions, professionally established and managed under the Sri Srinivasa Education Trust, constituted on 28th September 2001. The institution is committed to providing quality education and creating opportunities for students, particularly from rural communities. Our mission focuses on promoting health awareness, cleanliness, disease prevention, and community well-being. We strive to empower rural communities by improving access to quality education and skill-based learning. Through academic excellence, values, and social responsibility, Subash Group of Institutions is dedicated to building a healthier, educated, and empowered society.

Which programmes do you offer?

We offer B.Sc. Nursing, M.Sc. Nursing, PB B.Sc. Nursing, GNM Nursing, Diploma in Pharmacy, B.Sc. Medical Imaging Technology, Diploma in O.T. Technology and Diploma in Ophthalmic Technic.

How do I apply for 2026–27?

Fill in the application form online, or download it and submit it. Then call our admissions team to confirm your seat. Seats are limited.

What are the eligibility rules and fees?

These differ for each programme. Please open the programme page, or call +91 96204 09905 and our team will explain the details for your course.

Is hostel accommodation available?

Yes. We have a residential campus with safe and comfortable hostel facilities.

Will I get hands-on clinical training?

Yes. Our attached teaching hospital gives students hands-on clinical postings from the early semesters.

Do you help with placements?

Yes. A dedicated placement and career guidance cell supports students.

Are the courses recognised and affiliated?

Our courses are university-affiliated and recognised. Full details are on the Affiliation page.
