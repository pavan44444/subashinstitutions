About the programme

The Diploma in Operation Theatre Technology (DOTT) is a two-year academic programme that introduces students to the fundamentals of discipline, ethics, layout and equipment in the operation theatre.

It is meant for candidates who want to learn how to handle and shift patients to wards and the recovery room, understand sterilisation methods, care for patients in emergencies and maintain records.

What you will study

The curriculum focuses on subjects such as:

  • Anatomy & Physiology
  • Basic principles of elementary nursing of patients
  • General pharmacology, including the pharmacology of anaesthesia, sedative / hypnotic and life support drugs
  • First aid techniques & procedures
  • Operation theatre & emergency care equipment management

Eligibility criteria

  • Senior Secondary (10+2) with Biology or Life Sciences as one of the subjects, with a minimum of 50% marks.
  • International applicants: a minimum of 50% in the qualifying examination, having studied the prerequisite subjects for the programme.
