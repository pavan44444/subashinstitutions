About the programme

The M.Sc. Nursing programme is designed to develop the right attitude in students and give them the knowledge and skills to recognise and intelligently interpret the physical, mental and emotional signs of illness, and to plan individual nursing care based on each patient’s unique needs.

It also builds the ability to work with other specialist medical and paramedical personnel, to devise and demonstrate disease prevention and health promotion programmes, and to follow the principles of hygiene and the ethical and moral codes of the profession. At a higher level, the programme builds the tools to carry out research and to apply advances in science and technology to nursing.

Programme objectives

  • To instil in students an attitude for nursing, and to give them the knowledge and skills to provide comprehensive nursing care to patients in all types of illness.
  • To develop skill in recognising and interpreting the physical, mental and emotional signs of illness, and to plan individual nursing care based on each patient’s unique needs.
  • To help students work in cooperation with medical and paramedical personnel, and take part in programmes for the prevention of disease and the promotion of health in the community.
  • To help students integrate the principles of hygiene and the ethical and moral code of nursing practice, and to prepare them for teamwork and rewarding careers worldwide.
  • To develop individuals who are sensitive to the rights of others and conscious of their duties and responsibilities to their profession, fellow citizens and the country.
  • To build the ability to research and to apply advances in scientific knowledge to the relevant fields of work.

Specialisations

  • Medical Surgical Nursing
  • Obstetrics & Gynecology Nursing
  • Pediatric Nursing
  • Community Health Nursing
  • Psychiatric Nursing

Eligibility for admission

  • A pass in B.Sc. Nursing (Basic), B.Sc. (Hons) Nursing, Post Basic B.Sc. Nursing or Post Certificate B.Sc. Nursing, or an equivalent qualification, with a minimum of 55% in aggregate marks.
  • Registration with the State Nursing Council as a Registered Nurse and Registered Midwife.
  • A minimum of one year of working experience.
  • Candidates must not have reached the age of 45 years on the last date fixed for submitting applications to the PG Degree Entrance Test conducted by RGUHS.

Career scope

Nursing professionals are needed in public and private sectors across the world. As India emerges as a health-care tourism destination, employment opportunities for professional nurses will keep growing. Graduates can work in:

  • Hospitals, day care, health care and residential care services
  • Nurseries, primary schools and family centres
  • Voluntary organisations
  • Corporate houses, resorts and retreats
  • Opportunities abroad, on a global scale
