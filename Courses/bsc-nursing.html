B.Sc. Nursing

Undergraduate degree programme in nursing.

Duration: 4 Years + 1 Year Internship Minimum 45% in Physics, Chemistry, Biology Affiliated to RGUHS
🎓

Affiliated & recognised

Affiliated to Rajiv Gandhi University of Health Sciences (RGUHS) and recognised by the Govt. of Karnataka.

✅

Council approved

Approved by the Karnataka Nursing Council (KNC) and the Indian Nursing Council (INC).

🩺

Internship included

A 4-year programme followed by 1 year of internship, open to Indian and international students.

Eligibility for admission

For both Indian and international students:

  • A pass in PUC / 10+2 or equivalent, with a minimum of 45% aggregate marks in Physics, Chemistry and Biology.
  • Candidates must have completed 17 years of age by 31st December of the year of admission.

Career scope

Nursing professionals are needed in public and private sectors across the world. As India emerges as a health-care tourism destination, employment opportunities for professional nurses will keep growing. Graduates can work in:

  • Hospitals, day care, health care and residential care services
  • Nurseries, primary schools and family centres
  • Voluntary organisations
  • Corporate houses, resorts and retreats
  • Opportunities abroad, on a global scale
