The bedrock of dedicated, grassroots patient care.

GNM is a 3-year diploma programme structured to equip students with core nursing skills, bedside care techniques, and community-centric health management experience.

Course Duration: 3 Years + Internship Diploma Programme
🏥

Practical Training

Extensive clinical postings in medical-surgical wards, emergency departments, and community health centers.

📋

Eligibility Criteria

10+2 from a recognized board preferably with science subjects (English minimum 40% aggregate).

🌍

Immediate Employability

High demand across private clinics, nursing homes, government health missions, and international healthcare providers.

🤝

Community Focus

Training specialized in maternal care, child health, vaccination drives, and rural health awareness programs.

Core Competencies

  • Deliver safe, effective, and empathetic bedside nursing care across general medical and surgical wards.
  • Assist physicians and specialists during diagnostic procedures, minor surgeries, and emergency resuscitations.
  • Manage routine maternal and neonatal healthcare services effectively within community setups.
  • Educate families and local communities on sanitation, hygiene, nutrition, and preventive healthcare.
