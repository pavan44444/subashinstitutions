We train people who stay calm when it matters.

M.Sc Nursing is designed to develop professional attitudes, advanced clinical knowledge, and research capabilities to excel in modern healthcare environments.

Course Duration: 2 Years Affiliated to RGUHS
🏥

Specialisations Offered

Medical Surgical, Psychiatric, Pediatric, Obstetrics & Gynecology, and Community Health Nursing.

📚

Eligibility Criteria

Pass in B.Sc Nursing with minimum 55% aggregate. Must be a registered Nurse & Midwife with 1 year experience.

💼

Career Scope

Limitless global opportunities across public/private hospitals, corporate healthcare houses, research, and educational sectors.

⭐

Recognised Credentials

Approved by the Indian Nursing Council (INC), Karnataka Nursing Council (KNC), and Govt. of Karnataka.

Programme Objectives

  • Instill and develop an attitude for comprehensive nursing care across all types of illnesses.
  • Build advanced skills in recognizing and interpreting physical, mental, and emotional manifestations.
  • Work seamlessly in cooperation with medical and paramedical personnel for disease prevention.
  • Integrate high standards of medical ethics, moral codes, and research methodologies.
