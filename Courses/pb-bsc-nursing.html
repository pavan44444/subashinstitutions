Upgrade your clinical expertise and career horizons.

Post Basic B.Sc. Nursing is a specialized 2-year undergraduate program tailored for Registered Nurses (RN/RM) holding a GNM diploma who want to upgrade their professional qualifications and leadership standing.

Course Duration: 2 Years For GNM Diploma Holders
📈

Advanced Learning

Bridges foundational diploma training with advanced nursing concepts, maternal-child health, mental health, and nursing administration.

📝

Eligibility Criteria

Pass in GNM (General Nursing and Midwifery) with registration as a Registered Nurse and Registered Midwife in the State Nursing Council.

🌐

Career Growth

Unlocks senior supervisory positions, administrative posts, and eligibility for pursuing specialized postgraduate M.Sc. degrees.

💡

Research & Practice

Introduces students to scientific methodologies, evidence-based nursing practices, and contemporary healthcare trends.

Key Focus Areas

  • Enhance professional competence and analytical capability in complex clinical decision-making.
  • Develop skills in educational methods and curriculum planning for teaching nursing students and junior staff.
  • Foster leadership qualities required to manage hospital wards, departments, and community health projects.
  • Encourage active participation in community-based health promotion and disease control frameworks.
