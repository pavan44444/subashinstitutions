PB B.Sc. Nursing

Post Basic B.Sc. Nursing programme.

Duration: 2 Years Open to Indian & international students Affiliated to RGUHS
🎓

Affiliated & recognised

Affiliated to Rajiv Gandhi University of Health Sciences (RGUHS) and recognised by the Govt. of Karnataka.

✅

Council approved

Approved by the Karnataka Nursing Council (KNC) and the Indian Nursing Council (INC).

🌍

Open to all

Admission is open to both Indian and international students who have passed GNM and are registered nurses.

Eligibility for admission

For both Indian and international students:

  • A pass in General Nursing & Midwifery (GNM).
  • Must be a registered nurse.

Course content

  • Nursing Foundations
  • Nutrition & Dietetics
  • Biochemistry
  • Biophysics
  • Psychology
  • Microbiology
  • English
  • Sociology
  • Medical & Surgical Nursing
  • Maternal Nursing
  • Child Health Nursing
  • Community Health Nursing
  • Mental Health Nursing
  • Introduction to Nursing Education
  • Introduction to Nursing Administration
  • Introduction to Nursing Research & Statistics
