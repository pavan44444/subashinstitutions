About the programme

Pharmacy is the medical field that deals with the preparation, distribution and appropriate use of medicines and drugs for therapeutic purposes. A Diploma in Pharmacy is an undergraduate diploma course in Pharmaceutical Science.

If you are curious about the principles of dispensing prescription medicines, and about ways to develop effective immunisation and health programmes, this diploma is an ideal place to start.

Why pursue a Diploma in Pharmacy?

Almost everyone has held a prescription and spoken to a pharmacist. Pharmacists do much more than give out medicine: they also work on new medicines and research immunisation. With the growing need for healthcare, career opportunities in pharmacy are increasing, especially for people with a knowledge of medical science and good communication skills.

  • Interest in science: If you are drawn to how drugs work in the body, to veterinary pharmacy or to pharmaceutical research, this diploma lets you help people use medicines properly, for humans and animals.
  • A passion for connecting with people: Pharmacists link the doctor’s prescription with the patient’s use of medicine and provide patient-centred care. They also work with scientists and doctors to develop new pharmaceutical practices.
  • Flexible career opportunities: The options range from nuclear pharmacy, which uses radioactive drugs to treat cancer, to veterinary pharmacy, which develops medicines for animals.

Eligibility criteria

  • The candidate must be at least 17 years old.
  • 10+2 or equivalent with Science subjects (English, Physics, Chemistry and Biology or Mathematics) from a recognised or accredited institution.
  • A minimum of 50% aggregate marks.
  • IELTS / TOEFL score.
  • Official transcripts of academic qualifications.

Advancing your career

After the diploma, students can move on to a bachelor’s or master’s degree in pharmacy, which builds a deeper understanding of the core concepts and a solid career in the field. Bachelor’s options include:

  • Bachelor of Pharmacy (B.Pharm / BS Pharm)
  • B.Pharm (Hons.)
  • Bachelor of Pharmacy in Ayurveda
