Hostel Facilities

Subash Group of Institutions operates its own campus hostel facilities for students across its courses. Separate hostel accommodation is available for boys and girls.

Hostel amenities include drinking water, dining facilities and mess services. Resident students remain under the supervision of the hostel warden and administration.

Hostel Rules & Regulations

Students seeking hostel accommodation must follow the prescribed procedures and regulations of the hostel administration.

  • Students must follow the admission procedures prescribed by the hostel administration.
  • Hostel amenities include drinking water, hygienic food arrangements, a dining room and mess facilities.
  • Resident students remain under the supervision of the hostel warden and administration.
  • Hostel authorities may inspect rooms and belongings at reasonable times.
  • Students must follow hostel policies, disciplinary rules and the prescribed timetable.
  • Visitors are permitted only on Sundays between 3:00 PM and 5:00 PM.
  • Students must obtain prior permission from hostel authorities and inform their parents or guardians before staying outside the hostel.
  • Outsiders are not permitted to stay in the hostel.
  • Alcohol, cigarettes and drugs are strictly prohibited.
  • Violations of hostel rules may result in termination of hostel accommodation.
  • Hostel and mess fees must be paid on or before the 5th of every month.
  • Non-payment of hostel fees for two consecutive months may result in termination of hostel accommodation.
